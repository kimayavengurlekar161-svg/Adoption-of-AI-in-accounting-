# Adoption-of-AI-in-accounting-
🤖 Adoption of Artificial Intelligence in Accounting

📘 About the Project

This project explores how Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming modern accounting systems. It studies awareness, benefits, and challenges of AI among students, teachers, and accounting professionals through surveys and field visits.


🎯 Objectives

🔍 To study the role and use of AI in accounting.

📊 To analyze awareness, benefits, and challenges among students and professionals.

💡 To understand how AI improves accuracy, efficiency, and fraud detection.

🧠 To suggest ways to promote AI adoption and training in the accounting field.

🧰 Tools Used

📋 Google Forms → For survey creation and data collection.

📈 Google Charts & Graphs → For analyzing and visualizing results.

🌐 Internet & AI Tools (ChatGPT) → For research, content organization, and secondary data.

🧭 Methodology

Conducted a Google Form survey among students, teachers, and professionals.

Collected both primary data (survey) and secondary data (books, reports, websites).

Analyzed responses using graphs and simple interpretations.

📊 Key Findings

92% of respondents are aware of AI in accounting.

Main benefits: accuracy, time-saving, and fraud detection.

Main challenges: high cost, job insecurity, and lack of training.

80% believe AI training should be included in accounting education

🧾 Conclusion

AI is revolutionizing accounting by automating tasks and improving accuracy. ⚙
However, awareness and training must increase to ensure effective use. 🎓
Future accountants must learn AI tools to stay relevant in the digital era. 💼
